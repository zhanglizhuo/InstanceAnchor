Interpretation:
  The full-sample ρ=+0.286 is NOT a weak positive signal. It is a TB-driven
  artifact: TB is the only dataset with significant positive Δ AND the
  highest Mantel/CKA/ml_degree. Excluding TB reverses the sign (Mantel
  +0.286 → −0.143; CKA +0.286 → −0.071). There is no portable predictor
  of where instance-level agreement will help. The class-prototype level,
  despite being the right granularity per SV, does not descend to a
  usable instance-level predictor in this dataset family.
